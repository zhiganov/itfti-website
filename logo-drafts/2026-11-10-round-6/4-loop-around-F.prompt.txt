Redraw the maze seal in the reference image with the same look: thick red square frame, red walls on warm off-white paper, vermilion ink with a slight hand-pressed irregularity at the outer edges, and the large letters I, t, F, t, I (capital I, lowercase t, capital F, lowercase t, capital I) as the main maze walls. Change the maze itself: the two capital I's now touch the left and right sides of the frame along their whole height, so there is NO corridor running up the left or right edge. The entrance is a gap in the middle of the bottom of the frame and the exit is a gap in the middle of the top of the frame. The route makes a long detour: from the entrance it goes left under the first t, up between the first I and the first t, across the top of the whole seal to the right, down between the second t and the last I, back left under the second t and the F, and finally up between the F and a t to the exit. Exactly one continuous white corridor connects the entrance to the exit; every other corridor ends in a dead end. All corridors have the same width. Symmetric only as a mirror image, never by rotation. NO hooked crosses, NO swastika-like shapes, NO pinwheel arrangements. NO hands, NO coins, NO Chinese or Japanese characters, NO other letters, NO signature, NO date.
