A single pictographic sign, as if it were one character from an ancient carved writing system, but invented and original. It combines an open cupped hand underneath and a small sprouting seed above it, joined by one curved line that returns from the sprout back down to the hand, forming a loop. Drawn with even, monoline strokes like a carved stone sign. Warm black on a cream background. NO Egyptian cartouche, NO Eye of Horus, NO ankh, NO pyramids, NO papyrus, NO hieroglyph rows: one sign only. It is a logo mark for ItFtI, a small open project that helps people turn their work into real-world impact and helps support flow back to people doing that work: impact leads to funding, funding leads to impact. Centered on a plain flat background, generous empty space around the mark, full-bleed, no frame, no border, no margin lines. Flat vector-like logo, crisp edges, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no photo, no mockup. It must read clearly at 32 pixels. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO WORDS anywhere.
