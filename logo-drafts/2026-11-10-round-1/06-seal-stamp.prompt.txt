The red ink is flat and even, with only a very slight hand-pressed irregularity at the edges. Vermilion on white. NO Chinese or Japanese characters, NO calligraphy, NO kanji, NO dragon, NO border pattern. Centered on a plain flat background, generous empty space around the mark, full-bleed, no frame, no border, no margin lines.
