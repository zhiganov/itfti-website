A single symmetrical glyph, mirrored exactly around a vertical axis, like one sign from an invented ancient script. The left half is a stroke rising upward and the right half the same stroke descending, the two meeting at the top and bottom so the sign reads the same from both sides, suggesting an exchange that flows both ways. Built from three to five bold strokes only. Deep indigo on an off-white background. NO Egyptian imagery, NO cartouche, NO Chinese or Japanese characters, NO runes copied from a real alphabet. It is a logo mark for ItFtI, a small open project that helps people turn their work into real-world impact and helps support flow back to people doing that work: impact leads to funding, funding leads to impact. Centered on a plain flat background, generous empty space around the mark, full-bleed, no frame, no border, no margin lines. Flat vector-like logo, crisp edges, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no photo, no mockup. It must read clearly at 32 pixels. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO WORDS anywhere.
