# Code of Conduct

This applies to collaboration on the ItFtI profile app and participation in its
hosted spaces.

- Treat people with respect. Critique work and claims without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, consent and other people's boundaries.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or sexually target others.
- Do not publish private contact details or other people's confidential material.
- Do not use the app to impersonate people or misrepresent their participation.

## Report a concern

Contact the project maintainer at **hello@citizeninfra.org**. Include the public
page, issue or comment URL and a short description. Avoid sending private
evidence in an initial report.

The maintainer can remove project comments, restrict participation or suppress
a profile on this host. The account owner controls their source records in
ATProto. Moderation here cannot delete copies held elsewhere.

Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and its context. The
maintainer should explain the action to the people involved where doing so
does not disclose private information or create further harm.
