# itfti-website

Landing site for itfti.org, joining two projects:

- [Impact to Funding](https://github.com/zhiganov/impact-to-funding): an agent skill that helps people put their work towards real problems, understand what difference it makes and find support to keep going.
- Funding to Impact: its funder-side sibling.

The site isn't built yet.

## Logo

`logo/itfti-seal.jpg` is the chosen mark: a red seal stamp whose letters I, t, F, t, I form the walls of a maze, with an entrance at the bottom and an exit at the top. The maze stands for the search for support, where many projects get lost before they find their way to funding. It is an AI-generated draft. `logo/itfti-seal.svg` is a Potrace trace of it, and `logo/itfti-seal-2048.png` a large PNG render.

`logo-drafts/` holds the exploration rounds that led to it, each with its prompts and generator script.
